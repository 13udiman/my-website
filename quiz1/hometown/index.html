LOCATION

East Java

Indonesia

HISTORY

A City With History

Surabaya is one of the largest cities in Indonesia and has an important place in the history of Indonesian independence.

The city is widely known as the City of Heroes because of the important events that took place during the struggle for independence.

ABOUT THE CITY

Why Surabaya?

Surabaya is a busy metropolitan city with a combination of modern urban life, historical places, local culture, and diverse culinary traditions.

CULTURE

Local Culture

Surabaya is known for its strong local identity, distinctive East Javanese culture, and its friendly but straightforward character.
